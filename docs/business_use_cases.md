# Business Use Cases

CreditRisk AI is designed for integration into multiple financial verticals.

## 1. Traditional Banking Underwriting
Automating the first level of loan assessment. The platform can filter out 60-70% of low-risk/high-risk applications, allowing human underwriters to focus only on "Medium Risk" cases.

## 2. Digital Lending & Fintech
Enabling "Instant Loan" features in mobile apps. The low latency of the FastAPI/XGBoost stack allows for sub-second approval decisions during the user journey.

## 3. Credit Card Issuance
Screening applicants for new credit lines based on their current leverage and historical repayment patterns.

## 4. Micro-Finance Institutions (MFIs)
Providing a data-driven risk framework for smaller lenders who lack the infrastructure to build their own scoring models.

## 5. Fraud Screening
Identifying applicants who provide suspicious data (e.g., extremely high income with high debt but low credit score) which might indicate identity theft or "income puffing."
