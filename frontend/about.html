About the Project

The Credit Risk Prediction Platform is an enterprise-grade AI solution designed to automate and optimize credit underwriting. Built with precision and transparency, it uses advanced Machine Learning to assess loan applications in real-time, ensuring safe and profitable lending decisions.


Core Technology

  • V4 Intelligence Engine: High-accuracy XGBoost classifier calibrated for realistic probability scoring.
  • Hybrid Rule Engine: Combines ML with strict banking underwriting standards.
  • Explainable AI: Provides clear, human-readable reasons for every risk decision.
  • High-Fidelity Data: Trained on over 120,000 synthetic records simulating real-world financial stress and stability.
